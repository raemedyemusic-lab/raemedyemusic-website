About Rae Medye

Artist Biography

Rae Medye is an independent Afro-Fusion and Afrobeat artist whose music blends inspiring lyrics, soulful melodies, and contemporary African rhythms. Through every release, Rae Medye aims to spread hope, peace, and unity while creating music that connects with listeners around the world.

The debut single, Peace Be Unto You, reflects a message of encouragement, faith, and positivity. Rae Medye continues to develop a unique sound that combines meaningful storytelling with modern Afro-inspired production.
